      How the numbers are worked out

      Source
      Google Sheet "SVAMITVA", Sheet1. One row per village per update day. Add new rows with a new Date and the dashboard picks them up when it is opened or refreshed.
      Stages
      Registry initiated → Patwari verification → Submitted → Registration → Completed. The sheet counts each plot in one stage only.
      Not started
      Plots minus the plots in all five stages.
      % completed
      Completed plots ÷ total plots.
      Change
      Completed plots now minus completed plots on the previous update day in the sheet.
      Moved
      A village where any stage count changed since the previous update day.
      Top and bottom
      Patwaris ranked on the measure picked on the Top & bottom tab, between the trend from and to dates. Bottom patwaris are listed lowest first.
      Work on a day
      Plots completed on that update day minus plots completed on the update day before. Faster means more work than on the previous day, Slower less, No work none.
      Stuck
      A village with no change for 3 or more updates in a row that is not yet 100% completed.
